Comparative Analysis of LLC, DAB, and Hybrid LLC–DAB Bidirectional DC–DC Converters for 2.2 kW EV On-Board Power Applications

Guna S G, Deepa Kulkarni, H S Yashaswini, Harshitha R Hedge, Prof Satya Prasad.

IEEE CONECCT 2026 · July 2026

Comparative analysis of 2.2 kW LLC, DAB, and Hybrid LLC–DAB bidirectional DC–DC converters for EV on-board power applications using MATLAB/Simulink. The study evaluates efficiency, output power, output current, THD, and Zero-Voltage Switching performance across 10–100% load conditions, achieving peak efficiencies of up to 96.5%.

View abstract

In electric vehicle (EV) and medium-power auxiliary applications, where high efficiency and steady operation over broad load ranges are necessary, isolated DC–DC converters are essential. Three separate converter topologies the LLC resonant converter, the Dual Active Bridge (DAB) converter, and an LLC–DAB hybrid architecture all intended for a rated power of 2.2 kW are compared using simulation in this paper. To ensure fair comparison, MATLAB/Simulink is used to model and evaluate the converters under identical electrical specifications. For load conditions ranging from 10\% to 100\%, performance metrics such as output power, output current, and efficiency are examined. Additionally, each converter's power quality is assessed using Total Harmonic Distortion (THD) assessment, and soft-switching behavior is methodically investigated using Zero Voltage Switching (ZVS) analysis. The LLC–DAB hybrid topology offers better performance balance across the whole operating range, making it a promising candidate for auxiliary DC power applications and electric vehicle applications. This paper highlights the trade-offs between efficiency, area, and power quality. Index Terms LLC Resonant Converter, Dual Active Bridge (DAB), Soft-Switching, Zero Voltage Switching (ZVS), Bidirectional Power Flow, Total Harmonic Distortion (THD), Electric Vehicles, Hybrid Converter
